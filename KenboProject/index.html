Kenbo Services Limited is an engineering company based in Kampala, Uganda. The company supports residential, commercial, community and infrastructure projects with construction, technical works, consultation and supplies.

Our priority is simple: understand the work, plan it well, deliver safely, and keep clients informed from start to finish.

  • Site-focused engineering and construction support
  • Road, bridge, building, water and electrical works
  • Practical solutions for private, commercial and community projects
