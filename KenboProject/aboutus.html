About the company

Engineering work built on trust.

Since 2009, Kenbo Services Limited has supported clients with practical construction, technical works, consultancy and project supply services.

Who We Are

Kenbo Services Limited is a Kampala-based engineering company serving clients across civil engineering, road construction, plumbing and water works, electrical works, environmental management, mechanical works, bridge works, shallow well drilling and general supplies.

The company works with engineers, technicians and support teams who understand that every project needs a balance of good planning, dependable workmanship and clear communication.

Whether the work is a building, road, bridge, water system or supply assignment, our aim is to help clients move from idea to finished result with confidence.

How We Work

Kenbo combines site experience with a client-first approach to make projects easier to plan, manage and complete.

Understand the Need

We begin by clarifying the site condition, client goal, expected quality and available budget.

Plan the Work

We help define the scope, materials, team requirements and practical delivery approach.

Deliver Responsibly

We focus on safe execution, clean communication and durable outcomes clients can rely on.

Our Promise

Kenbo Services Limited is built for clients who need engineering support that is practical, responsive and accountable. We keep the work grounded in real site requirements and aim for results that hold up after handover.

  • Professional handling of construction and technical assignments
  • Respect for safety, time and project requirements
  • Sustainable practices where the project context allows
  • Reliable communication before, during and after the work
